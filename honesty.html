Part 1

What is honesty?

Honesty means we tell the truth, say "I did it," and give things back.誠實，就是說實話、做錯了會承認、不是自己的東西會還回去。

Part 2

Honesty Words

Game 1

Honest or not?

Look and listen. Is it honest?看一看、聽一聽：這樣誠實嗎？

Game 2

What do you say?

Pick the honest words.選出誠實的說法。

Game 3

Build the sentence!

Listen, then tap the words in order.先聽一聽，再依照順序點單字。
